# Cybeersym — the thesis

*The navigable defense of the project, and the record of our own reasoning (including the failures).
For the single-file long-form, see [`THESIS.md`](../../THESIS.md). Every claim in this set is bounded
by **[05 — limits & honesty](05-limits-and-honesty.md)** — if you read one page, read that one.*

## The one-sentence claim

**Cybeersym is a wind tunnel, not a crystal ball:** an agent-based, stock-flow-consistent simulation
for testing whether *structural* hypotheses about inflation survive contact with real data — with
econometrics as the referee, not the engine — aimed ultimately at a **regime / domain-of-validity
classifier** (diagnose the dynamical class, don't just locate the equilibrium).

## The single honest headline

The project is, today, a **representational and methodological** contribution with one narrow
empirical foothold (egg-timing, out-of-sample). The claim that it is *more informative than the
standard toolkit* is **argued, not yet demonstrated** — the non-circular demonstration is
[future work](08-future-work-roadmap.md). We keep that line bright on purpose.
