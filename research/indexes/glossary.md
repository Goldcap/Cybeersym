# Glossary

| Term | Working definition | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Attractor | A set toward which nearby system trajectories evolve. | [Taxonomy](../notes/concepts/taxonomy.md) |
| Escape dynamics | Trajectories that leave a bounded region or grow without a finite attracting state. | [Hyperinflation](../notes/concepts/hyperinflation.md) |
| LTV | Labor theory of value; a family of theories relating value to socially necessary labor. | [LTV](../notes/concepts/ltv.md) |
| MMT | Modern Monetary Theory, emphasizing monetary sovereignty and real-resource constraints. | [MMT](../notes/concepts/mmt.md) |
| Provenance | Traceable origin and transformation history of a claim, artifact, or decision. | [Schema](../schemas/research-record.schema.md) |
