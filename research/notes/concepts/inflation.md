# Inflation

## Working definition

A sustained increase in a broad price level, distinct from a one-time relative-price adjustment.

## Mechanisms to separate

- Demand, supply, and administered-price effects
- Monetary and fiscal accommodation
- Wage-price and expectation feedback
- Exchange-rate and external-balance constraints
- Distributional conflict and sectoral bottlenecks

## Modeling caution

Do not infer mechanism from the aggregate price index alone. Track persistence, breadth, sectoral composition, institutions, and feedback loops.
