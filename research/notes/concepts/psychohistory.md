# Psychohistory

## Role in the project

Use Asimov's fictional psychohistory as a provocation about population-scale prediction, not as evidence that precise social forecasting is possible.

## Productive questions

- Which aggregate regularities survive individual unpredictability?
- How do reflexivity and policy response invalidate forecasts?
- What uncertainty bounds and forecast horizons are honest?
- Can models classify regimes without predicting exact events?

## Guardrails

Avoid false precision, unfalsifiable narratives, and claims that erase agency or institutions.
