# Modern Monetary Theory (MMT)

## Working frame

Separate operational claims about sovereign currency systems from policy prescriptions. Model nominal financing capacity alongside real resources, inflation, external constraints, institutions, and political limits.

## Questions

- Which claims depend on exchange-rate regime, currency denomination, or external debt?
- How should consolidated-government accounting coexist with institutional detail?
- What closes the model: taxation, interest policy, quantity adjustment, prices, or rationing?
