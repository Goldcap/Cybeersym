# Hyperinflation

## Research frame

Test whether hyperinflation can be represented as an escape regime: reinforcing feedback pushes nominal variables beyond a bounded attractor while institutional and real constraints reshape the path.

## Candidate feedbacks

- Fiscal financing and declining real tax revenue
- Currency substitution and falling money demand
- Exchange-rate pass-through
- Shortening contracts and indexation
- Political legitimacy and supply collapse

## Falsification needs

Specify observables that distinguish escape dynamics from structural breaks, explosive-but-transient adjustment, or data-scaling artifacts.
