# Labor Theory of Value (LTV)

## Scope

Treat LTV as a family of claims, not a single proposition: theories of value substance, price regulation, exploitation, and reproduction need separate statements and tests.

## Modeling questions

- What is the unit of socially necessary labor time?
- How are heterogeneous labor, capital goods, and joint production handled?
- What mapping is claimed between labor values and observed prices?
- Which results are definitions, accounting identities, causal claims, or tendencies?
